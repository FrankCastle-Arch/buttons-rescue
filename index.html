His story

A calm companion with a curious streak.

Eight months is a long time to wait for the right person to notice you. Buttons has spent that time figuring out exactly what he likes: a quiet lap, and a sunny windowsill for long naps. He's not the type to warm up to just anyone right away — but give him time, and you'll earn something better. You'll earn a faithful sidekick. All he needs now is a calm home and someone patient enough to let that bond grow on its own time.

Urgent: Buttons is a long-stay resident.

How to adopt Buttons

  • Ask your household if everyone is ready for a cat.
  • Visit Marmalade Nook and spend some time with Buttons.
  • Complete the adoption form with a shelter helper.
